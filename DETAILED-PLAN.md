### Why Shopify (Not Custom Code)

After researching every option, **Shopify** is the right choice for you because:

| Factor | Why It Matters for You |
|--------|----------------------|
| **No coding required** | You can update products, prices, and content yourself |
| **24-48hr setup** | Not months of custom development |
| **Built-in B2B apps** | Wholesale pricing, NET30 terms, PO acceptance via apps |
| **SEO-friendly** | Built-in blog, meta tags, sitemaps, fast loading |
| **Mobile admin app** | Manage your store from your phone while traveling |
| **App ecosystem** | 16,000+ apps for any feature you need later |
| **Proven for medical supplies** | Other medical equipment companies use it successfully |

## 9. Risk Management

### What Could Go Wrong & How to Handle It

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| AI creates inaccurate product content | Medium | High | Every product description gets your approval before publishing |
| Social media post feels "too AI" | Medium | Medium | Use your real photos/videos — authenticity is built in |
| Blog posts rank slowly at first | High | Low | SEO takes 3-6 months. Consistency wins. Don't stop at month 2 |
| VizoCare notices and responds | Low | Low | Good — it means you're competing. Stay focused on YOUR strengths |
| Tool costs increase | Low | Low | Total is still < $200/mo. Switch tools if needed — nothing is locked in |
| Content quality varies | Medium | Medium | OpusClip/InVideo both have free tiers — test before committing |
| Shopify prohibited product issue | Low | High | PPE (gloves, masks) are generally allowed. Verify at signup |
| Make.com automation breaks | Medium | Low | Make.com has error notifications. Fix or rebuild quickly |
