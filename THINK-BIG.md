### The Subscription Model

This is where it gets big. Medical supplies are **consumables** — they run out. Every customer is a repeat customer.

```
AUTO-REORDER SUBSCRIPTIONS:
═══════════════════════════

  "Never run out of gloves again."

  Customer signs up:
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                                              │
  │  Product: Nitrile Gloves (Medium)            │
  │  Quantity: 10 cases                          │
  │  Frequency: Every 30 days                    │
  │  Price: $XX.XX/case (5% subscription discount)│
  │  Payment: Auto-charge or invoice NET30       │
  │                                              │
  │  [Start Subscription]                        │
  │                                              │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  What happens:
  • Day 1: Order placed automatically
  • Day 1: Ships from your warehouse
  • Day 2: Customer receives it
  • Day 25: Claude emails: "Your next shipment is in 5 days.
            Need to adjust? Reply to this email."
  • Day 30: Auto-reorder fires
  • Repeat forever.

  WHY THIS IS HUGE:
  • Predictable monthly revenue (you know exactly what's coming)
  • You can negotiate BETTER wholesale prices with YOUR suppliers
    because you can guarantee volume
  • Customer never has to think about it
  • Switching cost is high — once they're subscribed, they stay
  • VizoCare doesn't offer this.
```
